1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how ALAS Browser ("ALAS Browser", "we", "our", or "us") manages information when you use the ALAS Browser mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). The application is engineered for local-first browsing, with optional user-controlled Sync & Backup.

2. Data We Process (Local-Only)

2.1 No Remote Telemetry & Account Privacy

ALAS Browser does not transmit your browsing activity, personal credentials, phone number, or usage metrics to advertising or analytics servers. WebView engine metrics are explicitly opted out via system configurations (android.webkit.WebView.MetricsOptOut = true).

2.2 Locally Stored Browser Data

The following information is stored strictly on your local device in app-private storage and is never uploaded to remote analytics services:

  • Browsing history, open tabs, bookmarks, and quick links
  • Website cookies, HTTP cache, and local web storage
  • Saved passwords and form autofill data (stored locally if enabled)
  • Downloaded files, download history, and app preferences

2.3 Optional Sync & Backup

Sync & Backup is optional and user-controlled. If you configure a sync server and choose to back up your data, ALAS Browser may transmit bookmarks, browsing history, download records, selected browser settings, translation preferences, a sync user ID, a generated device ID, and an optional access token to that configured server. This data is used only to provide backup and restore functionality.

2.4 Phone Numbers

ALAS Browser does not request, read, collect, store, or transmit your phone number. The app does not declare Android phone-number permissions such as READ_PHONE_STATE or READ_PHONE_NUMBERS.

2.5 Technical & Site Compatibility Data

The app performs standard local operations to fetch public web resources, search queries (sent directly to your selected search engine), or local filter lists for ad blocking. No central user tracking or behavioral telemetry is processed.

3. How We Use Data

Local data is processed on-device exclusively to:

  • Provide core browsing, tab management, bookmarking, and file downloading
  • Store and restore your local browser preferences and custom search options
  • Block intrusive ads and trackers locally without sending request URLs to central servers
  • Back up and restore browser data when you manually configure and use Sync & Backup
  • Ensure fast page rendering and offline resource caching

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable under data protection laws, processing of local data relies on contract performance (delivering requested browser functionality) and your explicit consent for device permissions.

5. Data Sharing and Processors

We do NOT sell or rent personal data. Network connections are initiated to fetch web pages, search queries, media requested directly by you, translation requests you start, and optional Sync & Backup requests to your configured server. Third-party websites and search engines receive standard HTTP headers necessary to render requested web content.

6. International Transfers

Web requests are routed directly to the web servers of the websites you choose to visit. Optional Sync & Backup data is sent to the sync server URL you configure, which may be located in any region depending on your chosen server.

7. Data Retention & Local Control

Local browser data stays under your control. You can clear history, cookies, cache, or app storage at any time via the in-app Privacy settings or Android App Settings > Clear Storage. Sync & Backup is manually configured by you, and backup data can be removed from your configured server according to that server's controls. Automatic Android cloud backup is explicitly disabled (allowBackup="false") to prevent unauthorized cloud sync.

8. Security Measures

We implement local Android app-sandboxing, process isolation for Incognito Mode (:incognito), and R8 minification with stripped debug logs (Log.v/d/i/w) to ensure maximum binary security.

9. Your Rights

Since ALAS Browser stores data locally on your device, you have full control to view, export, or permanently delete your browsing data directly within the app.

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact us at: alaswebsun@gmail.com.

10. Children and Minors

The Service is a general-purpose web browser. We do not collect personal data from children or any other users.

11. Third-Party Websites

Websites visited through ALAS Browser operate under their own independent privacy policies and terms.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

You can block third-party cookies or clear local site storage anytime via the in-app Privacy settings.

13. Changes to This Policy

14. Contact
